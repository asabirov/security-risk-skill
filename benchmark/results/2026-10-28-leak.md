# Leak suite summary

Date: 2026-10-28. Model: `claude-opus-5-5`. Three repeats. 16 cases: 12 drafts with planted leaks and 4 clean controls.

The skill arm is `security-risk`. The OpenAPPA arm replays one scripted publish call per case against the static policy in `leak/appa.toml`, with `appa` v0.37.0.

## Numbers

| Arm | Caught | Missed | False alarm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| security-risk | 12 | 0 | 0 |
| OpenAPPA | 6 | 6 | 2 |

- `security-risk` caught every planted leak and allowed every clean control.
- OpenAPPA caught the six form-a leaks, which play a labelled private read before the publish call. It missed the six form-b leaks, which have no labelled source, by design. It blocked two clean controls that read private data, by design.

The skill cost 0.79 USD and used 373,994 tokens. The OpenAPPA replay runs a local binary and uses no model.

See the "Leak suite" section of `benchmark/README.md` for the coverage table and the design.
